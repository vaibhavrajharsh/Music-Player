FAQs

How to play songs? +
Click on any song from your library or playlist, then press the play button. You can pause/resume anytime.
How to create playlists? +
Go to your library, click on "Create Playlist", give it a name, and start adding your favorite songs.
How to control volume? +
Use the volume slider at the bottom of the player or use your device’s volume buttons.
Can I shuffle songs? +
Yes! Click the shuffle icon on the player to play songs in random order.
How to add songs to favorites? +
Click the heart icon next to any song to add it to your favorites list.
